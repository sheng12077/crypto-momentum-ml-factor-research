# Step 1: research-input readiness protocol

Registered 2026-11-18 before implementation and data probes. Scope authorized by
user: historical Size coverage/identity, contract lifecycle and funding accounting.
Project stops at historical trading simulation and OOS review; paper/live deferred.

Expected: existing price/funding observations alone do not establish an eligible
historical universe. Public market-cap history may have limited coverage, differing
supply definitions, revisions and uncertain historical availability. Additional
official futures events should reduce the priority evidence queue without resolving
every contract episode or proving point-in-time announcement availability.

Acceptance gates:
- Preserve all original releases and their hashes. Write NEW diagnostic artifacts.
- Test public/no-key sources only, capped responses and bounded pilots; no paid
  subscription, credentials, purchase, scraping past access restrictions or full
  vendor backfill. Save source URLs, retrieval time, raw SHA256 and declared limits.
- Market-cap pilot: explicit UTC dates, stable vendor IDs and metric definition,
  exact raw values, duplicate/nonpositive/missing detection. A reported success
  means only available pilot data parsed; not 924-asset, lifetime or PIT coverage.
- Map all924 as a REVIEW queue, not an eligible universe. Never join by ticker
  alone, auto-strip 1000 prefixes, substitute current supply/rank or zero-fill.
- Review the 38 currently unsourced priority IDs against official FUTURES notices;
  retain unavailable/ambiguous sources as unresolved. Never use spot delist times.
- Funding: audit exact event timestamps/intervals and boundaries on bounded samples,
  distinguish rate observation from cash settlement and historical announcement
  availability. No settlement prices inferred from OHLC; no expected PnL/returns.
- No factor values/rankings, model fitting, labels, backtests or OOS performance.
  Metadata coverage over2025-26 may be inspected, not strategy outcomes.
- Deliver executable tooling, focused checks, coverage/error summaries, readiness
  matrix and next actions. Full history acquisition needs a separately reviewed
  source/coverage plan. Unknown facts remain unknown; no automatic strategy gate.

## Proposed sequence

1. Use2021–2022as the development/search period. Record every candidate,code and
   input version,model hyperparameters,and failed run;retain losing results.
   Selection within development is legitimate,but presenting only winning
   trials as if they were prespecified would overstate the evidence.
2. Prefer an inner chronological check inside development(e.g.train2021,
   validate2022) to selecting from fitted2021–2022performance alone. This does
   not change the outer training window. Mature labels only;purge boundaries.
3. Before viewing2023performance,freeze the promoted candidate,the primary
   selection objective,acceptance/failure criteria,all benchmark implementations,
   execution/cost/funding rules and any model refit schedule. Refit on eligible
   2021–2022examples and evaluate2023. A fixed selection procedure is also valid,
   but its complete search budget must be declared before opening2023results.
4. Evaluate the promoted candidate against fixed benchmarks and the weekly
   no-buffer reference. Do not pick the best configuration from2023and call
   that same year's result independent confirmation. A good2023outcome supports
   continuation;it does not establish absence of overfitting or future profit.
5. If the candidate passes the predeclared criteria,keep the strategy/model
   specification fixed,refit using eligible2021–2023data under the frozen policy,
   then evaluate2024. If2023is used to revise the specification,mark it as used
   development validation;2024can be the next untouched check. Any use of2024
   for further choices also belongs in the development ledger.
6. Keep2025-01-01through2026-11-01exclusive sealed. Freeze all final rules/refit
   policies before its first performance inspection. If revised after inspection,
   that interval is no longer untouched evidence for the revised strategy.

  results. Do not change the objective to whichever metric flatters a winner.

# Full-universe retention screening protocol

Registered 2026-11-18 before implementation/execution, based on dde16bb.
User clarified: maximize usable retained assets, not the number of exclusions.
This is data-quality/identity screening, not strategy selection or an experiment.

Scope: all924 planned identifiers over2021-01..2026-10. Preserve delisted names,
unknown identities and all raw files. Exclude defective intervals/components as
narrowly as evidence supports; do not demand every identifier survive every gate.

Acceptance:

- Offline; no returns/factors/labels/fits/PnL, final OOS inspection or orders.
- Verify plan/download/audit/release hashes and counts, population consistency,
  prior decision policy and CMC provenance. No source artifacts overwritten.
- Quick full924-ID review queue distinguishes positive trade observations,
  three-series calendar overlap, Size identity leads and current type annotations.
  Do not discard current noncoin, delisted or missing-current-metadata identifiers
  by a retrospective membership rule. An unresolved class remains review-required.
- Detailed mode reads planned trade acquisition reports (22,635, expected from
  frozen plan), verifies their keys/identities against the plan, and uses their
  audited counts/positive-volume times. Do not reread every raw ZIP or run backtests.
  This trusts the earlier raw integrity audit; it is not new per-ZIP certification.
- Report exact active trade-file counts and month-level three-series availability;
  preserve zero-volume months/gaps as interval flags, never delete a whole asset
  because one month is defective. Include120-calendar-day elapsed-history diagnostic
  only, not proof of120 valid closes or a chosen warmup/rebalance rule.
- Size leads use literal symbols plus explicit reviewed overrides only. Retain
  ambiguities, provenance and unmatched cases; no blind prefix stripping, cap
  multiplication, future-coverage ranking or current membership selection.
  Counts are candidate/data-coverage diagnostics, never certified tradable counts.
- Apply named Size-join blocks and record scoped restrictions; no automatic joins
  approved. Do not treat a restriction affecting part of a month as whole-month
  or whole-history deletion. Final daily qualification requires row-level checks.
- Fixed real pilot: BTCUSDT,ETHUSDT,BNXUSDT,SUNUSDT,1MBABYDOGEUSDT across their full
  acquired months. Tests cover missing/stub months, renames/overrides, exclusion
  scope and metadata tampering. Successful resume reuses verified checkpoints.
- Handoff the larger metadata scan with copyable command, log/result paths,
  measured pilot timing/unknown full runtime, success criteria and compact JSON.
  No repeat downloads or paid data needed. Summarize full924 quick results now;
  clearly mark any detailed full scan not yet executed.

OOS stays sealed. Ex-post metadata screening and unresolved publication vintage
do not establish an unbiased historical trading universe. No final pool selected.

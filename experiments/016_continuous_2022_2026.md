# Experiment 016: uninterrupted 2022--October 2026 reconstruction

Registered 2026-11-22, after all annual results and final holdout were disclosed.
This is a retrospective implementation/accounting experiment, not a fresh OOS test.

## Expectation and fixed specification

A single Nautilus account should reproduce the frozen decision rules while retaining
actual quantities, funding liabilities and compounded cash across every year boundary.
Its result may differ materially from chaining independently liquidated annual runs.
No expected direction of return improvement is assumed. Do not optimize this experiment.

- Initial cash USDT 100,000, start 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC; last NAV snapshot
  2026-10-31 00:00 UTC; final liquidation 2026-10-30 23:00 UTC (legacy endpoint).
- Same conditional 37-identifier cohort and previously verified prepared inputs.
- Reuse the existing 2022--2026 annual LightGBM models/predictions; zero new fits.
  Verify model parameters, training-label availability and cached prediction equality.
  No Size, new feature, buffer, position filter or performance-based modification.
- Five features: momentum20/60/120, funding7, imbalance7; top/bottom five,
  equal +/-10% target NAV; minimum 20 candidates. Actual quantities held between decisions.
- Preserve two-week schedule anchored to the first Monday of EACH calendar year.
  This can produce a 7/21-day interval across years; do not silently rephase it.
- New annual scores take effect only on that year's scheduled rebalance. No Jan 1
  liquidation, cash reset, outside contribution, synthetic zero-return bridge or
  forced target reset. Liquidate only once at the registered final endpoint.
- Two cases: 5 and 10 bps adverse execution slippage; 5 bps perpetual taker fee,
  no BNB discount. Funding uses the existing completed-hour mark proxy, charged
  on the pre-fill position. Repeat 5 bps case independently for exact replay.
- BTC/ETH benchmarks: fixed spot units, one buy on Jan 1 2022 at 02:00 UTC and
  one final sale; 10 bps spot taker fee and 5 bps adverse slippage each side.
- Reuse official terminal boundaries and reject unknown settlement if held.
  Do not exclude assets or invent settlement prices. Momentum-only control is
  outside this experiment: its known EOS settlement blocker remains unresolved.

## Acceptance thresholds fixed before execution

Engineering pass requires complete daily calendar (1765 snapshots), one account,
no intermediate final events, no external cashflows, correct annual forecast/training
cutoffs, unchanged verified caches, all held marks/execution/funding proxies available,
ledger reconciliation <= USDT 0.01, independent source-price/fee/funding audit and
identical financial SHA256 on the primary replay. Any unsupported held terminal
settlement or source gap is a blocker, never a tradable proxy.

Descriptive economic gates (report individually, never change after observation):
primary net return > 0 and Sharpe_rf0 > 0; stress net return > 0; primary Sharpe
higher than both BTC and ETH; primary absolute MDD lower than both benchmarks;
compounded primary return excluding the best month > 0. These are screening gates,
not statistical significance or authorization for paper/live trading.

Report whole-path return/CAGR/volatility/Sharpe/MDD/Calmar, calendar-year slices
including the Jan 1 cross-year return, fees/funding/turnover, monthly concentration,
continuous NAV/drawdown plots, annual cached SHAP and explicit development status.
No unsupported claim of PIT universe, historical exchange margin/lot certification,
causal SHAP profit attribution, fresh OOS or live readiness.

User runs the full historical simulations. Preserve logs, contracts, attempts and
compact summary; no downloads or silent cache repair. Existing experiments untouched.
